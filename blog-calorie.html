Calorie and Weight Calculation System: The Complete Guide

Published by Calculater313.com Team

1. Overview

The calorie and weight calculation system is built to provide users with a reliable, scientifically backed estimation of their daily energy requirements. It serves as a foundational tool for achieving various health and fitness objectives, such as weight loss, weight maintenance, or muscle gain.

2. Core Scientific Formula: Mifflin-St Jeor Equation

The system utilizes the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is widely recognized by health professionals and dietitians as one of the most accurate formulas for calculating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)—the number of calories the body burns at complete rest to maintain vital functions.

  • For Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age in years) + 5
  • For Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age in years) − 161

3. Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

To account for daily movement and physical activity, the calculated BMR is multiplied by an Activity Factor to determine the Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE):

  • Sedentary (Little or no exercise): BMR × 1.2
  • Light Activity (Light exercise 1–3 times/week): BMR × 1.375
  • Moderate Activity (Moderate exercise 3–5 times/week): BMR × 1.55
  • Very Active (Intense exercise 6–7 times/week): BMR × 1.725
  • Extra Active (Strenuous daily exercise or physical job): BMR × 1.9

4. Weight Management Goals Integration

Based on the computed TDEE, the system automatically translates the energy balance into actionable weight goals:

  • Weight Maintenance: Consuming calories equal to the TDEE.
  • Safe Weight Loss: Creating a moderate daily caloric deficit of approximately 300 to 500 calories below TDEE.
  • Weight / Muscle Gain: Creating a caloric surplus of 300 to 500 calories above TDEE.
